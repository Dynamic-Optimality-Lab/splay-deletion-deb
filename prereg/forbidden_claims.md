- "Splay is approximately monotone" from finite data alone.
- "The conjecture is solved" without a Phase-15 universal proof.
- "`b_n*` is bounded" from an observed finite sequence.
- "The sequence converges, therefore Splay is dynamically optimal."
- "This state reveals the unique global potential formula" from `U = V`.
- "The Pair Access Lemma is proved" from enumeration survival.
- "H works for all n" / "dynamic optimality follows" for a candidate that
  merely survives finite tests (`CANDIDATE_H_SURVIVES_FINITE_TESTS` ceiling).
- Any theorem statement whose constant secretly depends on `n`.
- Presenting adversarial-test survival as proof (claim-level system forbids
  `UNIVERSAL_PAIR_ACCESS_LEMMA_PROVED` without Phase-15 symbolic proof).
